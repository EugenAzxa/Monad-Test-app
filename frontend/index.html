Notarize a document

We compute a keccak256 fingerprint of your file and record it on Monad with your address and a timestamp. The file itself never leaves your browser.

Verify a document

Inherit

A dead-man's switch for your estate.

Add encrypted documents and name your heirs. If you stop checking in, access is released to them — automatically.

1Create your vault

Set how long you can stay silent before your heirs gain access. Use 60 seconds for a live demo; weeks or months in real life.

2Set your encryption passphrase

Your documents are encrypted with a key derived from this passphrase (PBKDF2 → AES-GCM). It never leaves your browser and is never stored on-chain.

3Add an encrypted document

The file is encrypted in your browser, then its fingerprint and encrypted payload are stored on-chain. (Demo stores small files directly on-chain; production uses IPFS.)

4Name an heir

Enter your heir's wallet address and the public key they generated in the Claim tab. Your master key is wrapped (ECDH) so only they can unwrap it — and only after release.

Claim

Inherit what was left to you.

Generate your key, watch for release, then decrypt everything — all in your browser, no one else can.

1Generate your heir key

Creates an ECDH keypair in your browser. Share the public key with the vault owner. Save your private key — it's the only way to unlock your inheritance.

2Check a vault

Enter the owner's wallet address to see whether their dead-man's switch has released.

3Claim & recover documents

Once released, claim your inheritance. Your private key unwraps the owner's master key and decrypts every document — all locally.
