Protect

Prove it existed. Prove it's unchanged.

A permanent on-chain fingerprint of any document - impossible to forge or alter without anyone noticing.

Your document → Fingerprint on-chain → Provably the real one
Example: upload your will. We stamp a unique fingerprint of it onto Monad. Later anyone can prove your file is the exact, unaltered original - and spot a fake instantly.

Notarize a document

We compute a keccak256 fingerprint of your file and record it on Monad with your address and a timestamp. The file itself never leaves your browser.

Verify a document

Upload any file to check whether its fingerprint is on record - and exactly who registered it, and when.

Inherit

A dead-man's switch for your estate.

Add encrypted documents and name your heirs. If you stop checking in, access is released to them - automatically.

You store & encrypt → You check in → You go silent → Heirs get access
Example: you check in every month. If you ever stop - say, you pass away - then after the time you set, your children automatically receive the key to your documents. A "dead man's switch."

1Create your vault

Set how long you can stay silent before your heirs gain access. Use 60 seconds for a live demo; weeks or months in real life.

2Set your encryption passphrase

Your documents are encrypted with a key derived from this passphrase (PBKDF2 → AES-GCM). It never leaves your browser and is never stored on-chain.

3Add an encrypted document

The file is encrypted in your browser, then its fingerprint and encrypted payload are stored on-chain. (Demo stores small files directly on-chain; production uses IPFS.)

4Name an heir

Enter your heir's wallet address and the public key they generated in the Claim tab. Your master key is wrapped (ECDH) so only they can unwrap it - and only after release.

Contacts & receivers

Save people by email or phone, then choose who receives your documents. Contacts are private to your browser - never stored on-chain.

Endowment & $LEGACY

Deposit MON into your vault. It stays yours (withdraw anytime before release), earns projected yield while it sits, and is paid to your heirs as a life-insurance payout when the vault releases. Every deposit also mints $LEGACY, the ecosystem's economic unit. Testnet prototype - yield is illustrative.
